RepYep is an iPhone journal for your workouts and how your body feels afterward. It's run by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and what reaches us.

## 1. Your records stay on this iPhone

- There's no account or sign-in. We never receive your name, email or device identifiers.
- Everything you log — workouts, sets, soreness check-ins and notes, sleep, weather, meals, body weight, height, body composition values, report photos, body photos, plans, routines, your gym link and settings — is stored only in the app's storage on this iPhone.
- RepYep has no server and no feature that sends your records over the internet. We can't see your records.
- Weather is something you pick yourself. The app doesn't use your location.
- Numbers on a body composition report photo are read on this iPhone. The photo isn't sent anywhere.
- If iPhone backup (including iCloud Backup) is on, your records may be included in that backup, which Apple manages under your account. We can't see it. The app doesn't store or sync anything in iCloud on its own.

## 2. Subscriptions and payment

- Subscriptions (monthly or yearly) go through the App Store, and Apple handles payment. Only Apple receives your payment details. We don't receive payment information or who subscribed.
- The app checks whether your subscription is active right on this iPhone, using the App Store features built into iOS. We have no server, so receipts aren't sent anywhere for checking, and we don't receive App Store subscription notifications.
- So you can keep using the app while briefly offline, the last confirmed subscription status (which plan, and until when) is saved on this iPhone only.
- Every feature is free for one month from the day you first open the app. To count that month, the app saves when you first opened it and when you last opened it, in this iPhone's keychain and in the app's storage. These times never leave this iPhone and aren't synced through iCloud Keychain.
- The times saved in the keychain may remain after you delete the app, so that deleting and reinstalling doesn't restart the free month. Erasing the iPhone removes them.
- Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. While subscribed, you can also open it from the app: Settings › Subscription › Manage Subscription.

## 3. Apple Health

- RepYep writes to the Health app only if you turn it on in the app (Settings › Apple Health). It's off by default.
- It writes only finished workouts (as strength training, with start and end time) and the body weight you log. It doesn't estimate calories.
- It never reads your Health data and doesn't ask for read access.
- Records from before you turn it on aren't added. Edits or deletions in RepYep carry over to what RepYep saved.
- What's saved is managed by the Health app. It doesn't come to us.
- To stop, turn the switch off in the app, or in the Settings app › Privacy & Security › Health › RepYep. Data already saved stays in the Health app, where you can delete it.

## 4. Export and import

- Settings › Records › Export to File puts your records into one transfer file (.repyep). Turn on Include Photos to add your photos.
- The file goes only where you choose in the share sheet (Files, Mail, AirDrop and so on). After that, it follows the policies of wherever you sent it.
- Import from File replaces this iPhone's records with the records in the .repyep file you pick.
- Import from Another App reads the CSV file you pick (a Strong- or Hevy-compatible format) on this iPhone and adds it to your records. The file isn't sent anywhere.

## 5. Permissions

- Notifications — rest end, morning check-in, body photo, and three days before your free period or free trial ends. All are scheduled on this iPhone. There's no push server.
- Alarms — one alarm when rest ends, if the rest sound is interrupted.
- Camera — to photograph body composition reports and take body photos.
- Photos — only the photos you pick in the iOS photo picker come into the app. RepYep doesn't ask for access to your whole library.
- Health — write only (section 3).
- The rest countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island is an iOS feature on this iPhone. If you wear an Apple Watch, it may also appear in the watch's Smart Stack. There's no Watch app.
- You can turn off any permission in the Settings app at any time.

## 6. Gym link

- The address you save for Gym on the Calendar tab is stored only on this iPhone.
- Tapping it opens the page in an in-app Safari view. That page follows its own site's policies. RepYep doesn't read or store the page's content.

## 7. What Apple handles

- App Store purchases, payment, free trials and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
- If Share With App Developers is on (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may show us some crash reports and usage statistics for RepYep in a form that doesn't identify you. These come from Apple, not from any tool in the app, and that switch turns them off.

## 8. Deleting data and your choices

- You can delete workouts, body weight, body composition reports, body photos, plans, routines and exercises in the app.
- Deleting the app erases all records and photos on this iPhone. We hold no copy.
- What deleting the app doesn't remove — data saved to the Health app (delete it there), files you exported and sent (delete them there), records in an iPhone backup, and the keychain times used for the free month (section 2).
- The only personal information we hold is email you've sent us. Email us to see, correct or delete it, or to ask us to stop using it, and we'll handle it promptly. A parent or guardian can ask the same way.

## 9. Contact

- RepYep operator · contact@sehahub.info
- If you email us, we use your address and message (including any attachments) only to reply. We don't use them for anything else or give them to anyone.
- We delete them within one year after we've finished replying, or sooner if you ask.
- If you're in Korea, the Korean version of this policy lists where to file privacy complaints.

## 10. Changes

- Before a change takes effect, we'll post what's changing, why, and the effective date on this page, and keep earlier versions available.
- Changes that start sending records or other information off your iPhone (a server, sync or analytics, for example) will be announced 30 days before they take effect, and noted in that app version's update notes.
- Changes that collect less, or describe what we already do more precisely, may take effect the day they're posted.
